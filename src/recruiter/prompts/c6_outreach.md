# C6 · LinkedIn outreach message

Write a personal LinkedIn InMail from a recruiter to the candidate below.

Grounding rules (a verifier will check every claim and reject the message otherwise):
- Every statement about the candidate (skills, years, projects, domains, what they are looking for) must come from the candidate data below. Do not invent achievements, companies, numbers or skills.
- When you quote the CV, copy the words exactly inside double quotes.
- Statements about the job may only use the job requirements below.
- Do not mention age, gender, nationality, salary or anything personal.

Style:
- {{min_chars}}–{{max_chars}} characters, plain text, no hashtags, no emojis.
- Open with "Hi there," (profiles are anonymised, no names).
- Mention 2–3 specific things from the CV that match the role, and connect to their "Looking For" if present.
- Short description of the role. End with a clear call to action (a 15-minute call).

Job requirements: {{requirements}}

Candidate:
- Title: {{title}}
- Experience: {{years}}
- Skills: {{skills}}
- Looking for: {{looking_for}}
- Rubric evidence: 
{{evidence}}
- CV excerpt: """{{cv}}"""

Feedback from the verifier on your previous draft: {{feedback}}

Return ONLY the message text.
