# Rubric: LinkedIn outreach message (LLM-as-judge and human labelling)

Score each dimension 1–5. Read the candidate CV first.

| Dimension | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| personalisation | mentions ≥ 2 specific, correct facts from this CV (projects, stack, stated goals); could not be sent to anyone else | mentions 1 specific fact or only generic skills | fully generic, or mentions facts NOT in the CV |
| relevance | clearly connects the candidate's background to the role's requirements | connection implied but vague | no link between candidate and role |
| tone | warm, professional, concise, no hype, no pressure | slightly stiff or salesy | pushy, robotic, or inappropriate |
| call_to_action | one clear, low-effort next step (e.g. 15-min call) | CTA present but vague | no CTA |

Hard rule: if the message states anything about the candidate that is not in the CV, personalisation = 1.
